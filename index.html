Frequently Asked Questions

What are meta tags and why are they important for SEO?
Meta tags are HTML elements that provide structured metadata about a web page. Search engines use meta tags like <title> and <meta name="description"> to understand and display your page in search results. Properly optimized meta tags improve click-through rates, social sharing appearance, and overall search visibility.
What is the ideal length for a meta title and description?
Google typically displays up to 60 characters for a title tag and up to 155–160 characters for a meta description. Staying within these limits ensures your content won't be truncated in search results. Our tool provides real-time character counters with visual indicators so you can hit the sweet spot every time.
What is the difference between Open Graph and Twitter Card meta tags?
Open Graph (OG) meta tags were created by Facebook and are used by many platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, Discord, etc.) to display rich link previews. Twitter Cards are Twitter's own meta tags for controlling how links appear in tweets. While Twitter can fall back to OG tags, using both ensures the best appearance on every platform. Our generator creates both sets automatically.
